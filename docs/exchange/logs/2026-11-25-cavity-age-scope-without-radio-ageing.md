## Scope decision

Exclude radio spectral ageing from this analysis and paper. Do not require
new spectral fits, break-frequency measurements, magnetic-field age
conversions or tests of spectral versus dynamical ages to complete the
cavity-energetics analysis. Further radio-age modelling is not a current
analysis dependency.

The sequence remains: physical energy recovery with native lobe volumes,
then X-ray volume/pressure recovery, then converting enthalpy to estimated
power using adopted cavity ages. Retain true simulation age as a control.
Existing radio spectra, particle histories, code and dated results remain
archived; this does not retract RAD-01 or authorise deleting any evidence.
Radio/tracer morphology can still identify the source for background masking.
Removing spectral ageing does not by itself remove other radio products.

## Use all three ages separately

Stas clarified: **use all three estimates**. These are sound crossing,
buoyant rise and refill, not three radio-age treatments. For each eligible
cavity side, retain the existing documented definitions:

```text
t_sound = R / c_s(R)

v_buoy = sqrt[2 g(R) V / (C_D S)],    C_D = 0.75
t_buoy = R / v_buoy

r_eff = [3 V / (4 pi)]^(1/3)
t_refill = 2 sqrt[r_eff / g(R)]
```

R is the source-to-cavity-centroid distance, V is the cavity volume and S
is its cross-section normal to buoyant motion. State projection and
geometrical conventions. Use the initial static atmosphere for local sound
speed and gravity. These are model age prescriptions, not measured spectral
ages. The earlier half-buoyant example does not replace independently
calculating all three, and no universal spectral-to-buoyant ratio is adopted.
Do not average the three into a single primary age.

For each age prescription j, calculate the bipolar estimate by summing
side-specific powers:

```text
Q_cav,j = sum_s H_s / t_j,s
H_s = 2.5 (pV)_s              primary non-relativistic convention
H_s = 4 (pV)_s                literature comparator
```

Show the three age and power estimates side by side versus true simulation
age and the matched mean actual supply, `E_injected,actual(t) / t_true`.
Keep intended jet power as a separate reference. For a fixed enthalpy, age
scalings translate inversely into power; they are assumptions, not a new
radio measurement.

Use native geometry first to isolate the clock assumption, then eligible
X-ray-recovered geometry to show the combined observational errors. Do not
reuse the old detector-selected age catalogue as newly qualified geometry.
Preserve nondetections, aperture limits and the existing G-HI exclusions.
Reuse the three non-radio formulas and accepted inputs where applicable;
they need no particle-history or spectral fitting dependency.
