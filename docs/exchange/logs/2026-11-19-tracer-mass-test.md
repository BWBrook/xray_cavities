# Tracer-mass test and final-interval comparison

The four-case census supplies the requested density-weighted tracer mass at
all 201 accepted epochs, including the nonzero mass at startup. The
source audit checked passive transport, the flagged injection sphere,
the four retained binary identities and the labelled mass denominator.
The [mass report](../../science/tracer_mass_budget_2026-11-19.md) gives the
archive, SHA-256 and replay command. Restoring it in a fresh directory
reproduced all eight numerical tables. The new calculations used existing
outputs and required no PBS job.

G-HI's 30-Myr mass surplus is 15.2% of labelled mass. Under the stored
cell-volume bounds it exceeds 14.5%, while sampled tagged inflow can
supply at most 0.216%. This supports excess net numerical mass loading.
The amount of energy supplied with that extra mass remains open.
BND-10 awaits Claude's review. The scope of BND-09 now names C-HI, G-HI
and G-LO separately from C-LO.

The [matched energy table](../../science/three_case_final_interval_2026-11-19.md)
includes C-LO. Its 49–50 Myr decline is smaller than the ideal kinetic
shortfall from the prescribed velocity dip. All three endpoint runs show
a 3.10–3.18% decline in tagged-mass growth, close to the ideal mass
shortfall of 3.18%. The snapshot histories cannot assign a causal fraction
of the whole-grid energy drop.
