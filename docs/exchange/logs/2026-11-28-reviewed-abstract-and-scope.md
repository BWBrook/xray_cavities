## Abstract

Jet powers of radio galaxies in clusters and groups are commonly estimated from X-ray cavity enthalpy divided by an assumed age. We test that inference against four hydrodynamic simulations with labelled bipolar kinetic powers of 2 × 10⁴⁵ and 2 × 10⁴³ erg s⁻¹ in a cluster and a group atmosphere. We examine 19 case-ages from 10 to 50 Myr, with the high-power group represented through 40 Myr. We measure how much supplied energy the native lobe enthalpy represents, test whether an X-ray outline recovers enough of the lobe to estimate its enthalpy, and compare three cavity ages with true source age. We sum the side-specific powers and compare them with cumulative net gas delivery divided by elapsed source age.

We define the lobe as all cells with more than 0.1% jet material by mass and reconstruct the cumulative net gas energy delivered through the injector. Lobe enthalpy, 2.5pV, is 45–48% and 40–43% of delivery in the high-power cluster and group runs, and 58–99% and 54–68% in the corresponding low-power runs. Using 4pV multiplies each fraction by 1.6. At 30 Myr, 2.5pV is 43–81% as large as the signed energy change inside a sphere enclosing all gas moving faster than 100 km s⁻¹. The enthalpy-to-delivery ratio varies with power, atmosphere and age in these runs.

We froze two image-only rules, seed-and-grow and a 5% deficit below the fitted radial median, and applied them to 54 held-out noiseless 1-keV images at three side-on azimuths. Neither covered 75% of the projected lobe on any of 108 sides. Median area coverage was 23% and 42%, respectively. Across the 30 high-power cluster sides, the radial rule returned a median 1.08 times the native volume while covering a median 36% of the lobe area. In one viewing direction across 18 case-ages, restricting emission to within 10 kpc of the midplane raised the median fraction of lobe area dimmed by more than 5% from 42% to 91%. Each comparison used the undisturbed image at the matching depth. Initial light from beyond the slab contributed most to the change in mean brightness deficit at each case-age. Brightening of ambient gas also contributed.

All three clocks exceeded the true source age on every lobe side. With native enthalpy, median sound-crossing, buoyant-rise and refill powers across 19 case-ages were 20%, 25% and 9% of mean delivery. Neither image-only rule qualified a held-out outline for an end-to-end X-ray power estimate.
