# Midplane-slab pilot for G-HI at 20 Myr

Stas asked how much foreground and background gas hides a cavity in projection. This pilot compares the same pair of lobes along the native y axis at three emitting depths. The 0° full-depth image and its detector result are already known. The new measurements are the two thinner images and their matched time-zero and tracer comparisons. [Supervisor’s request](../exchange/logs/2026-11-25-midplane-slab-xray-cavity-request.md) · [Full-depth comparator](assets/ghi20_front_constraint_2026-11-25/area_scores.csv)

**25 November review addendum.** The original pilot below fitted the initial images but did not apply the detector to them. The review found false cavities in both undisturbed slabs. The four-case extension now runs an explicit initial negative control at every depth and uses brightness change against the matched initial image for its physical area comparison. [Four-case protocol](four_case_midplane_slab_protocol_2026-11-25.md)

**Emission.** Use G-HI output 20 (19.9996143 Myr) and its actual initial state, with the archived 1-keV emission prescription at redshift 0.05. The primary comparison uses the whole y domain and the intervals [−30, +30] and [−10, +10] kpc. The x–z sky grid is common to all images. Integrate the emissivity of each cell times the length of its intersection with the chosen interval. Include all gas in that interval and preserve the earlier treatment of emissivities outside the tabulated range. Record the affected cell counts. Reproduce the archived full-depth brightness at both epochs, tracer path length, and emission from the current tracer cells at both epochs before interpreting a thinner image.

**Detection.** Refit the unmasked radial background separately to each full or truncated image. The detector uses the original 180-kpc background aperture, 5-kpc annuli and 10-kpc smoothing. Select pixels with a fractional deficit greater than 5% inside 120 kpc, retaining eight-connected components of at least 25 kpc² within each hemisphere. The original exact-edge full-depth mask must reproduce pixel for pixel. Publish the radial profiles, fitting support and aperture contact. The direct fractional change against the matched time-zero brightness measures a separate, physically referenced depression on the same sky pixels.

**Lobe area and volume.** A sightline belongs to a tracer footprint when it intersects a positive length of gas with current jet tracer greater than 0.001. Integrate actual sky-cell areas, counting a sightline once. Use the full-depth tracer footprint as the common reference. Score each outline also against its own slab tracer. Report selected, overlapping, missed and false exterior area, completeness and purity for the two lobes separately, then sum physical areas before calculating bipolar fractions. Report the tracer area and volume retained by the slab. Measure the latter by integrating path length times sky area and compare it with an independent native volume sum.

**Foreground dilution.** On a fixed tracer footprint the full brightness is the sum of the slab and the two excluded wings along y. Compare integrated current and time-zero flux on exactly the same sightlines, with physical sky-area weights. The full-depth fractional depression is the mean of the slab and wing depressions weighted by each region’s share of initial flux. This distinguishes dilution by bright but unchanged material from changes in the removed gas itself. Emission surviving inside the slab can be divided between the current tracer cells and surrounding gas. Compare this physical accounting with changes in the fitted background and detected outline before drawing a conclusion about this case.
