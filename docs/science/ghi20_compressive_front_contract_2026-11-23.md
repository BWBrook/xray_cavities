# A native compressive layer in G-HI at 20 Myr

Prospective method for the exposed 0°, 45° and 90° side-on views of production output 20 (19.999614 Myr). The G-HI grid has 960 × 960 × 1,014 physical cells, spans ±200 kpc transversely and ±250 kpc axially, and uses the Stewart group atmosphere 002031. Runtime details, jet and output settings come from the [source-bound production receipts](assets/turn15_spherical_budgets_2026-11-05/first_comparison/summary_receipt.json). The original [shock validation failure](independent_bow_shock_validation_2026-11-04.md) stands. This experiment measures the extent and emissivity of a *currently compressing layer*. A smooth acoustic compression can qualify. A spatial coincidence cannot determine shock status, entropy production, or propagation speed.

## Cell selection

The only selection inputs are current pressure, density and three fluid velocities, initial pressure and density in the same cells, and the exact native cell faces. Let $P=\ln(p/p_0)$, $D=\ln(\rho/\rho_0)$, $n=\nabla P/|\nabla P|$ and $h_n=(\sum_i n_i^2/\Delta_i^2)^{-1/2}$. Subtracting the initial atmosphere in log space suppresses static stratification. Positive pressure and density are required. Use physical three-point, nonuniform, second-order first derivatives at the native centres. Define three dimensionless two-cell changes: $2h_n|\nabla P|$, $2h_n n\cdot\nabla D$, and $-2h_n\nabla\cdot v/c_s$, where $c_s=\sqrt{(5/3)p/\rho}$ in the same velocity units as the primitives. Require *all three* to exceed the following fixed cutoffs:

| Arm | Pressure | Density along pressure gradient | Convergence | Derivative neighbours |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Primary | 0.045 | 0.025 | 0.015 | ±1 cell |
| Loose | 0.020 | 0.010 | 0.0075 | ±1 cell |
| Tight | 0.080 | 0.040 | 0.030 | ±1 cell |
| Wide stencil | 0.045 | 0.025 | 0.015 | ±2 cells |

The wide stencil retains the same two-cell multiplier. The fixed two-cell derivative guard applies to all six faces and all arms. No derivative touches fictitious or ghost data. No shock inversion or entropy cut is applied. The layer includes its resolved thickness, rather than one extremum per column. A broad, sufficiently weak compression may fall below all cutoffs.

## Geometry and disambiguation

For an outward front, the compression increases the pressure towards the interior. Retain cells with $-n\cdot x>0$ outside the 0.75-kpc injector sphere. Construct 26-neighbour components over this complete three-dimensional detection domain. A component touching the tested edge (the innermost available derivative at any face) is boundary-associated, regardless of its size. Among other components, require at least 32 native cells and physical spans of at least 2 kpc along two Cartesian axes. Rank eligible components separately in each $z$ hemisphere by $\sum dV/h_n$ in that hemisphere. This is a resolved layer-area weight; a thick band can rank ahead of a thinner, longer sheet. Use the top-ranked component on that side, retaining its holes. A missing qualifying component gives an empty primary layer on that side. Record the other qualifying components, including their area weights and sizes, and project their union and the boundary-associated candidates separately. No interpolation joins disconnected sheets. This ranking is an operational identification, not proof of jet origin. External disturbances disconnected from a face can remain; projection cannot resolve all line-of-sight coincidences.

Project each native layer using the same existing exact-boundary line-of-sight overlap operator, sky bins and azimuth as the exposed X-ray products. The geometric path uses every selected cell, independently of tracer, and includes the lateral and axial surfaces. For the primary path also project $h_n$ and divide its integrated value by the path length. This local native width is distinct from the image pixel scale. Keep the 120-kpc observing aperture as a display and observer qualification only. The emission calculation then partitions cells into current $\mathrm{tr1}>10^{-3}$ lobe, current ambient in the primary layer, and all remaining current ambient. Compute both current and initial emissivity in precisely those cells for each component using the original retained-linear 1-keV model. Sum the three independently accumulated components and compare with the earlier whole-grid projection. The controlled replacement adds initial minus current emission of the ambient layer to the whole image. Original and refitted backgrounds are evaluated separately.

## Validation before native alignment

On the actual G-HI production axes, exercise uniform core and stretched outer patches at axial, transverse and oblique orientations. Require positive cases (a resolved finite-amplitude isentropic acoustic compression and exact shocks at Mach 1.5 and 3) at all three thresholds and both stencil scales, with a selected cell within two local normal widths of the plane. Exercise an additionally broadened smooth compression and weaker Mach-1.1 shock, reporting every sensitivity response. Pressure-balanced contacts at density contrasts 10 and 100, rarefaction and static stratification must select no cells. A pressure-balanced converging contact must also fail the joint criterion. Check connectivity/edge rejection using independent components, finite volume projections, and tiled versus unpartitioned classification on synthetic data. Any failure of a required comparison is recorded before native alignment can be interpreted.
