The C-HI control surface is broad enough for the next planning decision:

- Goals 015-017 make the refined projected-bin accumulator the preferred

  the historical empty-column projection failure while preserving X-ray sums to

  endpoint in all three views and support checks are clean.
- Goals 023-027 turn the X-ray/topology caveat into an explicit conditional
  control surface. The representative `1` percent residual-core mask classifies
  as `connected_envelope_single_system`; `0.5` and `2` percent support the
  narrow-threshold regime, while `3+` percent masks remain ambiguity bounds.
- Goal 009 gives a reviewable thermal and kinetic Level 3 closeout, with the
  gravitational component still blocked by the missing potential/profile
  convention.

This is a good control case. It is not a final observational product. The
conditional topology package sits beside the accepted Goal 005/007 endpoint
products and does not replace the tracer-defined ellipses or freeze final
observational cavity topology.

## Remaining Caveats

The active caveats are real, but they do not all block the same next action:

- Final observational endpoint topology remains not frozen. The connected-core
  result is a conditional residual-mask application, not a final X-ray cavity
  method.
- Sparse radio products remain diagnostic. They are useful provenance and
  scalar/morphology continuity checks, not validated radio surface-brightness
  observables.
- Level 3 gravitational energy remains `blocked_missing_potential`. This blocks
  full ambient-energy closure, not local comparison-case readiness.
- No `C-LO` or `G-LO` comparison case has yet been prepared or produced under
  the current accepted control contracts.
- Nothing in this package is publication-ready or manuscript-facing.

## Branch Decision

The strongest next move is:

```text
comparison_case_preparation_readiness_gate
```

That branch should be local and evidence-driven. It should map the accepted
`C-HI` contracts onto the planned comparison cases, identify exactly what must
be true before any `C-LO` or `G-LO` compute, and preserve the PBS stop rule.
It should not stage remote files, submit PBS, or claim a comparison result.

I do not recommend another C-HI late-age sample by default. The current
`46/48/49/50/51` surface is smooth enough for this pass and no single extra
late output uniquely closes a stated interpretive gap. I also do not recommend
defaulting to another local topology-only loop: Goal 027 is good enough as a
conditional control surface unless a later comparison-readiness pass exposes a
specific method blocker.

Radio/raytracing and gravitational Level 3 remain important branches, but they
are separate method lanes. They should be opened when the project deliberately
chooses to harden observables or energy closure, not as a substitute for the
comparison-case readiness step that the three-case plan now needs.
