# Fixed-background test of the G-HI/20 compression front

Patrick proposed clipping the existing 5% radial-deficit candidates to the independently mapped compressive front. The primary comparison uses the original orange outlines from the published 0°, 45° and 90° side-on panels of the 20-Myr G-HI snapshot. All three images and tracer outlines were already exposed. This rule was recorded before calculating the clipping scores. [Patrick’s handover](../exchange/logs/2026-11-25-q032-front-constrained-cavity-handover.md) · [Original panels and front definition](ghi20_compressive_front_comparison_2026-11-23.md)

1. Use the original candidate pixels, including all exterior selections. Hold image, background, threshold, aperture and component selection fixed within each before/after pair. The only operation is to intersect the saved candidate with the front interior. Keep surviving fragments. Use the independently measured primary native front for the headline and repeat with the previously defined loose, tight and wide fronts as a sensitivity check.
2. Work on the full bipolar detector before splitting into lobes. The magenta outer edge is the exposed pixel boundary of the projected dominant compression layer (positive native front path length). Use the region bounded by its outer edge, including occupied frontier pixels. Require one connected footprint clear of the detector and native box boundaries, enclosing the source between the four central pixels. Test connectivity and fill from the outer sky under both four- and eight-neighbour adjacency. If the source is exposed to the exterior, there are competing components, diagonal junctions or an ambiguous enclosure, record the view as unscored. Do not bridge gaps, take a convex hull, interpolate across indentations or impose a closing radius. The check concerns projected geometry.
3. Score against the full projected native lobe, defined by positive path through cells with jet tracer above 0.001. Integrate each detector cell with its true width × height. Report negative and positive jet-axis hemispheres and their physical sums. Report false area removed relative to previously selected area outside the tracer, true area lost relative to previously selected tracer area, completeness before/after relative to the entire tracer area, and any tracer area outside the front. Empty denominators are undefined.
4. For context apply the identical rule to the original blue seed/grow candidates, to the radial candidates on the exact-edge image, and to Patrick’s previously discussed residual orange arc on the front-emissivity-replaced image with its existing refit. Also carry forward the two archived DET-06 100 km/s central fits on the exact image. Verify each candidate against its previous mask or numerical receipt before interpreting it. Each comparison keeps its own background fixed. The original orange rule remains primary throughout.

These three azimuths project the same simulated pair of lobes. The test uses a simulation-derived outer bound. Estimating that bound from X-ray data and evaluating it on held-out observations are separate experiments.
